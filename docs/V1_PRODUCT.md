# No-Show Reducer v1.0 Product Specification

## Workbench

Appointment Reminder Planner

## Domain table

Reminder cadence

## Metrics

- No-show Rate
- Revenue Protected
- Consent Safety

## Domain rows

- Appointment types entered
- Baseline no-show rate entered
- Reminder cadence planned
- SMS/email templates generated
- Quiet hours checked
- Cancellation policy written
- Tracking log started
- Owner SOP exported

## Generated deliverables

- Cadence calendar
- Reminder templates
- No-show tracker CSV

## Validation gates

- SMS needs consent
- Quiet hours enforced
- Sensitive appointment details flagged
